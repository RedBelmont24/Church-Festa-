One parish, fourteen nations, one celebration.

Every year, our parish family comes together to honour the cultures, colours and traditions that make Holy Angels home for so many. This year's Festa brings dance, music, and dishes from across Africa and beyond ,all in one afternoon on the church grounds.

Good food, made by the community.

Every dish on sale is prepared by parish families sharing a taste of home. Come hungry — proceeds from food and drink sales go straight back into supporting our parish.

On the day

  • Food and drinks on sale from 12 noon
  • Dishes from across our represented nations
  • Music and traditional dance performances
  • Cultural dress encouraged

Plan your visit
